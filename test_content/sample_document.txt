LinkedIn Post Draft: Lessons from Building an Automated Pipeline

Building software is easy. Building software that runs reliably at 3am without you is hard.

After months of building our automated content pipeline, here are the three lessons that changed how I think about production systems:

1. Observability first. If you can't see what's happening, you can't fix it. Log everything. Trace every transaction. Alert on anomalies before users notice.

2. Fail gracefully. Every system will fail. The question is how. Design for retry, rollback, and human override from day one.

3. Test the edges. The happy path is easy. The race condition at midnight when three events fire simultaneously — that's where real bugs live.
